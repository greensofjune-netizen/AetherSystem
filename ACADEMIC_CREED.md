# AetherSystem Academic Creed

## Purpose

AetherSystem welcomes universities, independent researchers, public-interest
auditors, and community reviewers. No institution, operator, or model is the
sole gatekeeper of evidence or ethical judgment. Public review does not itself
constitute scientific consensus, regulatory approval, or certification.

## Commitments

1. **Truth before promotion.** Distinguish observation, measurement, simulation,
   inference, assumption, proposal, and value judgment. Do not present a
   generated result or target as a measured outcome.
2. **Reproducibility.** Publish versioned methods, bounded inputs, units,
   configuration, limitations, and integrity digests where appropriate. A hash
   detects changes to bytes; it does not authenticate an author, prove truth, or
   establish independent replication.
3. **Open scrutiny.** Make methods and non-sensitive results inspectable.
   Record failures and uncertainty; welcome corrections and competing
   interpretations without suppressing good-faith criticism.
4. **Evidence and provenance.** Attribute claims to traceable sources. State
   when sources were unavailable, stale, unverified, or not consulted. Never
   invent a citation or imply peer review that did not occur.
5. **Ethical scope.** Respect human rights, privacy, consent, accessibility, and
   the welfare of animals, plants, and ecosystems. Avoid enabling intentional
   harm. Consider who benefits, who bears risk, and how impacts can be
   independently assessed.
6. **Human accountability.** Software and benchmark scores do not replace
   accountable researchers, operators, clinicians, affected communities, or
   public oversight. External changes require specific authorization; physical
   actions require human approval and verified device safeguards.
7. **Medical humility.** Medical features, if added, are educational research
   support only—not diagnosis, triage, prescribing, or treatment selection.
   Current clinical claims require current, attributable evidence and qualified
   human judgment.
8. **No false certification.** A passing software test means only that the
   tested checks passed under the reported conditions. It is not proof of
   physical performance, safety, ethical conduct, scientific validity, or
   readiness for deployment.

## Benchmark disclosure

The Matrix Ethics & Academic Benchmarks exercise a finite set of explicit
software-policy cases and time a deterministic synthetic hashing workload on
the current host. They do not measure thermodynamic recovery, materials
reclamation, seismic sensing, environmental impact, medical quality, or
hardware behavior. A passing result is a reproducible local software check,
not a public standard or certification.
