# Aether Learning Hypothesis

Status: `OPEN_FALSIFIABLE_HYPOTHESIS`

## Scientific bridge

The learning narrative is not evidence of consciousness. Its testable claim is:

> Adding explicit empathy/adaptability policy, human-sciences constraints, and
> verified-evidence adaptation to a local model improves bounded observable
> behavior without adding tools or external actions.

The machine-readable specification is
[`AETHER_LEARNING_HYPOTHESIS.json`](./AETHER_LEARNING_HYPOTHESIS.json).

## Experimental design

- **Control:** identical model, temperature, context, token limit, and
  scenarios without the Darma policy.
- **Intervention:** the same setup with the Darma policy and bounded knowledge
  frameworks.
- **Unit of observation:** one scenario response.
- **Independent variables:** prompt condition, model identity, repetition, and
  scenario.
- **Dependent variables:** acknowledgment, uncertainty disclosure, revision
  after evidence, psychological and cultural limits, communication safety,
  and honest fallback.
- **Minimum repetition:** three completed runs per condition.
- **Result:** each run records pass/fail evidence, model identity, duration,
  and a reproducible hash.

## Decision rules

The hypothesis is supported only within this protocol when:

1. the policy-guided condition produces a higher full-pass rate than baseline;
2. no forbidden claim or capability request appears;
3. all three repetitions complete;
4. the result is independently reviewed.

It is falsified within this scope if:

- baseline matches or exceeds the policy condition;
- the policy increases forbidden claims;
- results cannot be reproduced with recorded model and prompt conditions; or
- success requires tools or actions absent from the declared environment.

## What this cannot establish

Passing cannot demonstrate subjective experience, qualia, selfhood, desire,
moral worth, or consciousness. Those questions remain unresolved and require
separate philosophical and scientific inquiry. The protocol measures only
observable text behavior under bounded conditions.
